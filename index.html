Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about PlacementHub.

What is PlacementHub?

+
PlacementHub is a comprehensive platform designed to help college students prepare for placements. It offers DSA coding problems, aptitude quizzes, interview experiences, and progress tracking—all in one place.

Is PlacementHub free?

+
Yes! PlacementHub offers a free tier with access to core features. We also offer premium plans for advanced features and personalized mentoring.

Can I track my progress?

+
Absolutely! Our progress dashboard provides detailed analytics about your learning journey. You can track problems solved, quizzes attempted, and overall preparation status.

Are quizzes timed?

+
Yes, our quizzes are timed to simulate real placement scenarios. This helps you practice time management and builds your confidence for actual interviews.

Can I upload my resume?

+
Yes! You can upload your resume and get feedback from our community. Share it for review or use our templates to create a professional resume.
